How to play

Your objective is to finish a 12-month mandate with a strong weighted board score. Losing security, productivity or board confidence can end your campaign early.

  1. Review the monthly incident and your metrics.
  2. Spend up to three management actions on controls, hires or initiatives. Some controls reduce incident impact, but may temporarily lower productivity.
  3. Choose an incident response. Responses have costs and consequences and do not consume an action.
  4. Close the month. Funding and payroll are applied at the start of the next month. Monitor recurring salaries, staff morale and control coverage.

Security is worth 28% of your score, compliance 21%, productivity 19%, board confidence 20% and morale 12%. Campaigns save in this browser.
